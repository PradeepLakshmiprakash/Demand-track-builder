When a step is missedthe escalation ladder
1 · A trigger firesEach red tag above is a trigger that is switched on for this account. The escalation takes that rule's responsible person and severity. One open escalation per demand and trigger.
2 · Level 1The responsible person is mailed what happened, the steps to take and the date to respond by, set by the severity. The demand owner and the Lead admin are copied.
3 · They respondWith a reason and an action: correct and resubmit · resubmit as is · ask for more time · revise the start date · close the demand · no further action once it has cleared.
4 · Level 2, if the date passesLeadership and the business unit's delivery head are informed. The same person still has to act and is reminded once a day.
Running throughoutat every step
BCM sheet importThe GTD admin team uploads the full sheet. Rows are matched by requisition ID, a [DM-…] prefix, or one clear match. A row that is not in the app is escalated to the GTD admin team.
Sheet and panelThe sheet lags the panel. Panel records move a demand forward the day they are entered; a sheet stage further on always wins.
Revenue lostFrom the requested start date, or the day after the leaver's last working day: bill rate × {{ hours }} billable hours × working days late, until the candidate joins.
Non-billable costProactive, non-billable positions cost the account from their start date at the rate card cost, until the demand owner marks the position billable.
